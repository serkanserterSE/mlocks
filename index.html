Mlocks Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Mlocks is a 3D building game with magnetic tiles for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: Mlocks does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Mlocks has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app works fully offline and does not send any data to us or to third parties.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps a few things only on your own device:

  • your saved build,
  • settings such as music on or off,
  • your best scores for the designs,
  • small technical logs used to make the app run smoothly (they never leave your device).

This information is removed when you delete the app.

Photos

Photo mode lets you take a picture of your build. The photo is only saved or shared when you choose to, using the standard iOS share sheet (for example AirDrop, Messages or Save Image). If you choose Save Image, iOS asks for permission to add the photo to your photo library. Mlocks never reads your photo library and never uploads photos.

Children

Mlocks is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal data, it collects no personal data from children.

Third-party services

Mlocks does not include third-party advertising, analytics or social media services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
